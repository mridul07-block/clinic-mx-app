# Finance: quick guide

## Where you start

After you sign in you land on **Collections** in **Finance**: the money taken today across all centres.

## Your tabs

On a phone, the bar at the bottom holds **Bills** (Collections), **Unpaid**, **Packages** and
**More**. **More** opens **Home**, which reaches everything:

- **Finance**: tabs for **Collections**, **Unpaid**, **Packages**, **Revenue** and **Refunds**.
- **Packages**: the list of patients' packages.
- **Prices**: the price list, package types and preset discounts.
- **Patients**: patient records.
- **Tasks**: your tasks and your role's.
- **Audit log**: every change to bills, payments, refunds, packages and prices.
- **Account**: your own sign in details.

## Everyday tasks

### Check today's collections

1. Open **Collections**. **Collected today, all centres** is the total.
2. Below it, read **Today by method** and the amount at each centre.
3. Payments undone and refunds given today are counted on their own lines, never in the total.

### Follow up unpaid money

1. Open **Unpaid**. **Patient balances** lists each patient by OP number, largest first.
2. A red **Over 3 unpaid sessions** tag marks a patient to follow up. It never stops booking or check in.
3. Under **Unpaid bills**, tap a bill number to open its plan and payment sheet.

### Give an Other discount

1. From **Unpaid**, tap the bill number.
2. Under the bill, tap **Apply a discount** and choose **Other discount**.
3. Choose **A percent** or **An amount**, fill it in, and write the **Reason**.
4. Tap **Apply discount**. Your name is recorded as the approval. Only unpaid bills take a discount.

### Check packages

1. Open the **Packages** tab. Each centre shows its active packages, those expiring soon, and **Expired with sessions left**.
2. Tap **Active packages**, **Expiring soon** or **Expired with sessions left** to see the list.

### Read revenue

1. Open **Revenue**. It counts payments received and not undone, less refunds, on the day each was recorded.
2. **Revenue by month against the year before** puts each month beside the same month a year earlier.

### Record a refund

1. Open **Refunds**. Type the **Bill or invoice number** and tap **Find**.
2. Check what was paid and already refunded.
3. Fill in **Amount in rupees**, choose **Refunded by**, and write **Why is it refunded?**
4. Tap **Record refund**. Your name is kept as the approval. Refunding all of it closes the patient's package.

### Download a CSV

1. On **Collections**, tap **Download the last 30 days (CSV)**. On **Revenue**, tap **Download by month (CSV)**.
2. On **Unpaid**, tap **Download (CSV)** beside patient balances or beside unpaid bills.
3. Files show OP and bill numbers, never patient names. Keep them on clinic devices.

## What the tags mean

- **Unpaid**, **Paid**, **Cancelled**: where a bill stands.
- **Active**, **Expiring**, **Used up**, **Expired**, **Cancelled**: where a package stands.
- **Over 3 unpaid sessions**: a patient owes for more unpaid sessions than the clinic's limit.
- **Inactive**: a price, package type or discount no longer offered.

## If something goes wrong

- Ask your Super Admin.
- First reload the page. If **Find** shows no bill, type the number exactly as printed on the bill or invoice.
- If you see **You do not have access to this page**, your role does not include it: ask the Super Admin.
