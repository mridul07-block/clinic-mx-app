# Physiotherapist: quick guide

## Where you start

You land on **My patients today**: your visits today, in time order, kept up to date.

## Your tabs

There is no tab bar yet. Tap **Home** at the top of My patients today for:

- **Tasks**: your tasks, most urgent first.
- **My patients today**: your day, and **Colleagues today**.
- **Calendar** and **Patients**: bookings and patient records.
- **Alerts**: open red flag alerts at your centres.
- **Red flags**, **Knowledge base**, **My leave**, **Account**.

## Everyday tasks

### Start a visit

1. Once the desk checks the patient in, tap **Start session**. Later, tap **Open session**.
2. If **Gate blocked** shows instead, send the patient to the desk.
3. To cover a colleague, tap **Start session** under **Colleagues today**. The note is then yours.

### Write the session note

1. The note fills in from last time and saves as you type.
2. Fill **Any new symptoms?**, **Since last visit**, **Pain**, **Treatment given**, **Response** and **Next session**.
3. **Same as last time** copies last session's treatment, home programme changes and next session. Check it.
4. Fill each ticked modality's parameters. If a **Precaution** shows, fill **Reason it is still given today**.
5. At sessions 1 and 4, record each baseline measure or tick **Not tested**.

### Close the session

1. If something went wrong, tick **Adverse event** and describe it. The consultant and the Clinical Director get a task.
2. Clear the list under **Before closing**.
3. Tap **Close session**. The note is signed.

### Check a patient's record

1. Tap the patient's name.
2. **Overview** has the summary card, **Notes** the signed notes, then **Documents** and **Alerts**.
3. **Progress** shows pain, strength tests and **Phases**. Mark each exit criterion **Met** or **Not yet**, then **Move to Phase _n_**.

### Add an addendum

1. Signed notes never change. On **Notes**, open **Addenda (_n_)** under the note.
2. Write it and tap **Add addendum**.

### Handle a red flag alert

1. An emergency is a red banner on every page. Tap **I’m reviewing**.
2. Open **Alerts** and follow the alert's action: **Refer**, or **Close with decision**.

### Work your tasks

1. In **Tasks**, tap **Take it** to make a shared task yours.
2. Tap **Close…**, write the **Decision**, then **Close with decision**.

### See your outcomes

1. On My patients today, tap **My patients’ outcomes**.
2. Choose **Last 30 days**, **Last 90 days** or **Last 12 months**.

## What the tags mean

- **Running late**: past the start time, not checked in.
- **Here _n_ min**: checked in and waiting.
- **With you since 10:05**: you started the visit.
- **Form**: a first visit's health form, for example Submitted or Link expired.
- **Session _n_ of _N_**: this session's place in the plan.
- **Covered by _name_**: a colleague writes this note.
- **Raised**, **Reviewed**, **Referred**, **Closed**: a red flag alert's progress.

## Not live yet

- Questionnaires wait for the clinic's instruments, licences and sign-off.
- Exercise videos wait for the clinic's video library.
- Part B consent waits for the clinic's consent texts.

## If something goes wrong

- Offline? Keep working. **Saved on this device** sends when you reconnect. If asked, choose **Keep my version** or **Use the saved version**.
- "Check the patient in first.": ask the desk.
- Anything else: your Centre Manager. Sign in problems: the Super Admin.
