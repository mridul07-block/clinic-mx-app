# Signing in and security: quick guide

For every staff member.

## Where you start

You start on **Sign in**. Then you land on your role's own screen, or on the page a link sent
you to.

## Finding your way

- **On a phone:** the bar at the bottom holds your role's main screens and **More** (Home).
- **On a tablet or computer:** the bar on the left holds every screen you may open. On a computer it
  also holds the **Centre** Today and the Calendar open on (if you work at more than one; signing
  out forgets it), **Search or jump to**,
  and your name at the foot, for **Account** and **Sign out**.
- **Search or jump to:** press **Ctrl K** (or **/**), type part of a screen's name or a patient's
  name or mobile, then press Enter.

## Your role

- Front Desk: **Today**.
- Physiotherapist: **My patients today**.
- Centre Manager: **Dashboard**, on its **Alerts** tab.
- Finance: **Finance**, on its **Collections** tab.
- Super Admin: **Overview**.

## Everyday tasks

### Sign in

1. Type your work **Email** and **Password**. Tick **Show password** to check what you typed.
2. Press **Sign in**.
3. If asked, open your authenticator app, type the **6 digit code** and press **Verify**.

### Set up two step verification

The Super Admin, Centre Managers, Physiotherapists and Finance must use it. Front Desk may turn
it on from **Account** on Home.

1. Install an authenticator app on your phone, such as Google Authenticator or Microsoft
   Authenticator.
2. On that phone, tap **Add to authenticator app**. On a computer, scan the QR code with your
   phone.
3. Type the code the app shows and press **Turn on two step verification**.

### Reset a forgotten password

1. On Sign in, tap **Forgot password?**, type your **Email** and press **Send reset link**.
2. Open the emailed link, type a **New password** of at least 12 characters and press **Save
   password**.
3. Every device is now signed out. Sign in again.

### Stay signed in on a computer

1. A computer signs you out after 15 minutes without use.
2. A minute before, a box warns you. Press **Stay signed in**.

### Let your notes save themselves

1. Notes save as you type. The word beside the section title shows how far it got.
2. If the connection drops, keep working. The bar says **Offline, changes will sync**.
3. If someone changed the same note elsewhere, choose **Keep my version** or **Use the saved
   version**.

### Work on a phone

1. Every screen works at phone size. Details open in a sheet from the bottom.
2. A phone stays signed in for 30 days from your password sign in, so keep a screen lock on it.

### Sign out

1. On Home or **Account**, press **Sign out**.
2. Changes not saved yet? Press **Stay signed in** and wait until you are online. **Sign out and
   delete them** removes them from this device for good.

## What the tags mean

- **Saving**, **Saved**: your note is on its way, or stored.
- **Saved on this device**: you are offline; it is sent when you reconnect.
- **Not saved yet**: the server cannot be reached; it keeps trying.
- "Your session ended": your access changed. Sign in again, or ask your Super Admin.
- "Velyx support access, read only": the Velyx support team is looking in.

## If something goes wrong

- Ask your Super Admin. Lost your phone? They press **Reset two step verification** on your
  staff page, and you set it up again at your next sign in. No reset email? Check spam, then ask.
- Velyx support is off unless the Super Admin turns it on, for at most 24 hours, with a
  reason. Support can look but never change anything, and cannot open clinical records such as
  notes and red flags. Every page they open is logged for the Super Admin to see.
