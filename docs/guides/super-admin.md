# Super Admin: quick guide

## Where you start

After you sign in you land on the **Overview**: every centre today, the last 7 days, the SOP targets and revenue.

## Your tabs

On a phone, the bar at the bottom holds **Overview**, **Alerts**, **Settings** and **More**. **More** opens
**Home**, which reaches everything:

- **Overview**, **Dashboard**, **Finance** and **Figures**: the numbers for every centre.
- **Alerts**, **Tasks**, **Today**, **Calendar** and **Patients**.
- **Staff**, **Centres**, **Team**, **Prices**, **Packages** and **Appointment types**.
- **Knowledge base**, **Red flags** and **AI preparation sheet**.
- **WhatsApp templates**, **Support access**, **Opening entries**, **Kiosk tablets** and **Account**.
- **Audit log** (with **Download (CSV)** for chosen days): every change and every opened record, newest first.
- **Data requests**: patients' requests for a copy of their data, a correction or deletion. Record
  one from the patient's record with **Record a data request**.
- **Settings**: the clinic's rules, such as minutes until running late. Open **Change**, enter
  the new value and tap **Save**.
- With the Treats patients tick, also **My patients today** and **My leave**.

## Everyday tasks

### Add or change staff

1. Open **Staff** and tap **Invite staff**.
2. Fill in **Email** and name, choose the **Role**, **Clinical ticks** (**Treats patients**, **Consultant**) and **Centres**. Temporary staff get an **Access end date**.
3. Tap **Send invite**. If the email does not arrive, open their page and tap **Copy invite link**.
4. To change someone, open their page and tap **Save changes**. Deactivate leavers there; nobody is deleted.

### Edit a centre

1. Open **Centres** and tap the centre, or **Add a centre**.
2. Change the details and tap **Save centre**. The code and appointment prefix never change.
3. Tap **Preview letterhead (PDF)** to check what documents print.

### Keep prices and packages

1. Open **Prices**. Use **Add a price**, **Add a package type** or **Add a discount**.
2. Tap a row to edit or deactivate it. A change applies to new bills only.

### Change a setting

1. **Appointment types**: tap a type, set **Length in minutes**, tap **Save appointment type**.
2. A physio's **Consultation fee** and **Appointment lengths** are on their staff page.

### Approve a knowledge base version

1. Open **Knowledge base** and tap the version tagged **Draft, awaiting approval**.
2. Read the **Import report** and the **Questionnaires** list.
3. Under **Approval**, tick the box saying you have read the report, then tap **Approve version**. Records already started keep their version.

### Check patient content

1. In **Knowledge base**, tap **Patient content** and use **Find a condition**.
2. A consultant who treats patients writes the draft and taps **Approve for patients**.

### Turn on support access

1. Open **Support access**. Enter **Hours** and a **Reason**, then tap **Grant support access**.
2. Tap **End support access now** when they finish. **Past grants** shows what they opened.

### Add opening entries

1. Open **Opening entries**. Under **New opening entry**, fill in the patient, centre, **Physio**, **Sessions left** and **Package expiry**, then tap **Add opening entry**.
2. Use **Correct** to fix one before its first session. After go live, tap **Close opening entries**.

## What the tags mean

- **Active**, **Invite sent**, **Deactivated**, **Access ended**: a staff member's access.
- **Live**, **Draft, awaiting approval**, **Superseded**, **Passed over**: a knowledge base version.
- **Ready to fill**, **Awaiting licence**, **Awaiting clinical sign-off**: a questionnaire.
- **On**, **Off**: support access.

## Not live yet

- WhatsApp messages are queued but not sent; the desk shows patients a QR code instead. **WhatsApp templates** lists each message as a draft.
- The AI preparation sheet stays off until the AI key and its blind test are done.
- Questionnaires wait for the clinic's instruments, licences and sign-off.
- Part B wording for Dry needling and the Zero Gravity Treadmill, and the diagnosis categories, wait
  for the clinic's texts and mapping.

## If something goes wrong

- Turn on **Support access** and contact Velyx Labs.
- Someone lost their phone? Tap **Reset two step verification** on their page.
- Every change is recorded with who made it, before and after. There is no screen to read it yet; ask Velyx Labs.
