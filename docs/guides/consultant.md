# Consultant: quick guide

This page adds what the Consultant tick gives you; read the physiotherapist quick guide first.

## Where you start

The same **My patients today**. A consultation shows **Start consultation**, and a review shows **Start review**.

## Your tabs

As in the physiotherapist guide. **Knowledge base** also holds **Patient content**.

## Everyday tasks

### Run a consultation

1. Once the patient is checked in, tap **Start consultation**. It needs the submitted health form.
2. Work through **Subjective**, **Screening**, **Objective**, **Assessment** and **Plan** with **Next** and **Back**.
3. Everything saves as you type. To come back, tap **Open consultation**.
4. On **Screening**, confirm every item. If you find a red flag, tap **Red flag found**.

### Write the plan of care

1. On **Plan**, fill **Goals** (**Add as a goal** copies the patient's own), interventions, modalities, **Sessions a week**, **Weeks**, **Home programme** and **Review date**.
2. A modality with a precaution needs **Reason to keep it**.
3. Choose the **Treating physio**.

### Sign

1. Tap **Review and sign**. **Before signing** lists anything missing, with a link to it.
2. Tap **Sign and finish**, then **Sign**. Later changes are addenda.

### Explain to patient

1. Choose the diagnosis on **Assessment**.
2. Tap **Explain to patient** and show the patient your screen.
3. Tap **Back to the consultation**.

### Review the session 4 reassessment

1. You get a task when the treating physio records it. Tap **Open the reassessment**.
2. Write your **Decision** and tap **Sign decision**. The task closes.
3. Other tasks about your patients, such as new symptoms or an overdue review, also come to you. Close each with a decision.

### Review and discharge

1. Tap **Start review** on a review visit, or **Review and discharge** on the summary card.
2. **Final testing**: retest each measure, then tap **Sign final testing**.
3. **Confirmed diagnosis**: confirm or change the diagnosis, then tap **Confirm diagnosis**.
4. **Readiness**: the app lists the criteria. Mark each **Met**, **Not met** or **Not assessed**, or use **Override** with a reason. Then sign each status under **Clearance**.
5. **Discharge**: pick the **Discharge reason**, mark the **Goals**, then tap **Discharge**.

### Approve patient content

1. Open **Knowledge base**, then **Patient content**. Use **Find a condition** or the filters.
2. Open a condition, write the four parts, then tap **Save as draft**.
3. Read it, then tap **Approve for patients**, or **Discard draft**.

## What the tags mean

- **Suggested**: the app proposes it; you decide.
- **Awaiting review**, **Signed**: a session 4 reassessment.
- **Signed**, **Not signed**: a clearance.
- **Draft awaiting approval**, **Approved**: patient content.
- **Plan proposed**, **Active**, **Under review**, **On hold**: where the episode is.

## Not live yet

- The AI preparation sheet (**Preparation sheet ready**) and **Recheck with findings** are off until the AI key and its blind test are done.
- **Explain to patient** and "About your condition" show only once a consultant approves the content.
- Diagnosis categories wait for the clinic's mapping.
- WhatsApp messages are queued, not sent. The desk shows the patient a QR code instead.

## If something goes wrong

- "Only a consultant starts a consultation.": ask the Super Admin to check your Consultant tick.
- "Your latest changes are still saving. Try again.": wait a moment, then sign again.
- Anything else: your Centre Manager.
