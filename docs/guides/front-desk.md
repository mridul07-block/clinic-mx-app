# Front Desk: quick guide

## Where you start

You land on **Today**: your centre's appointments today. The chips at the top count and filter.

## Your tabs

On a phone, the bar at the bottom holds **Today**, **Calendar**, **Patients** and **More**. **More**
opens **Home**, which reaches everything:

- **Tasks**: call tasks, most urgent first.
- **Today**: check in, **Walk in**, **New booking**.
- **Calendar**: each physio's day.
- **Patients**: find anyone by mobile or name.
- **Packages**: active, expiring and expired packages, with sessions left.
- **Prices**: the price list, read only.
- **Kiosk tablets**: set up or stop the desk tablet.
- **Account**: your details, two step verification.

## Everyday tasks

### Check a patient in

1. Press **Check in** on the row.
2. Compare the OP number, age and mobile ending, then tick **I have checked who this is (name and
   date of birth)**.
3. Press **Check in**. Wrong patient? **Undo check in**, the same day.

### Get the health form filled

For a first visit about a problem, press **Form and reports** on the row.

1. **Show QR**: the patient scans it with their phone camera.
2. No phone? **Open on tablet**. Needs help? **Fill in with the patient**, then **Open the form**.
3. **Add reports** for photos or PDFs.

### Set up the desk tablet

1. On the tablet, sign in and open **Kiosk tablets** from Home.
2. Press **Set up as kiosk and sign out**, then lock the tablet to the browser.
3. To stop it, open **Kiosk tablets** on another device and press **Stop kiosk**.

### Book a walk in

1. On Today, press **Walk in**, choose the **Appointment type**, then **Show free times**.
2. Tap a time. Search **Mobile or name**, or add a **New patient**.
3. Tick the identity box, then **Book and check in** (or **Add, book and check in**).

### Make a new booking

1. Press **New booking**, choose the **Appointment type** and **Date**, then **Show free times**.
2. Tap a time, find the patient and press **Book**, or **Add and book** a new one.

### Take a payment

1. After the consultation, press **Plan and payment** on the row.
2. Record the patient's choice with **Record choice**.
3. Under **Bills**, pick **UPI QR**, **Card** or **Cash** and press **Mark paid**. A mistake?
   **Undo payment**, the same day.
4. Once paid, **Book plan sessions**: **Check dates**, then **Book sessions**.

### Mark a no show

1. In the Calendar, open the appointment and press **Mark no show** (from 25 minutes after the
   start), then confirm.
2. In **Tasks**, **Take it**, call the patient, then **Close with decision**.
3. They arrive after all? **Undo no show**, the same day.

### Use the paper form

1. Keep printed copies at the desk: print **Paper health form and consent (PDF)** from Today
   before you need them.
2. If Clinic Mx is down, the patient fills in a paper copy.
3. Later, enter it with the patient through **Fill in with the patient**, and photograph the
   paper with **Add reports**.

## What the tags mean

- **Booked**, **Confirmed**: not here yet. **Running late**: 15 minutes past the start.
- **Here n min**: checked in, waiting that long.
- **In progress**: with the physio. **Completed**: done. **No show**: missed.
- **Unpaid ₹…**, **Over 3 unpaid sessions**: money owed; see **This episode’s bills** in the
  drawer. Never a block.
- **Gate blocked**: the session cannot start yet, for example the plan is not paid.
- **Consent declined**: speak to the patient.
- Form: **Not sent**, **Waiting for the patient**, **Submitted**, **Link expired**.

## Not live yet

- WhatsApp messages are not sent. Show the patient a QR instead.
- Part B consent waits for the clinic's consent texts.

## If something goes wrong

Ask your Centre Manager, then the Super Admin. If Today says "Not updated for n s", check the
connection and reload. If Clinic Mx stays down, use the paper form.
